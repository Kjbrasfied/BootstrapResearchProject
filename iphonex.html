    Released on November 3, 2017, the iPhone X was a major stepping stone for apple, paving the way for all the future iPhones to come and setting the new standard in mobile devices. Perhaps one of the biggest changes made to the iPhone was the elimination of the home button, replaced by a swiping mechanic. By swiping up from the bottom of the screen you could exit apps, or look at your opened dash board. The thumb ID system by this point was instead a face ID system. The cameras drastically improved and finally we received the ability to make our emojis talk through the "memoji" feature.
